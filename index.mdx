Kello is an AI sourcing agent for recruiters, founders and lean hiring teams: email a job description to [source@kello.ai](mailto:source@kello.ai) and Kello sends a list of **up to 20 people** who fit the role, usually in **about two hours**.

Kello then reaches out to the people you approve and sets up first conversations with the ones who say yes.

Kello (also called Kello Picks) works like the sourcer on your team. It reads your role, reads each candidate's profile the way a senior recruiter would, and learns from every Yes and No you mark.

<Tip>
Send your first role to [source@kello.ai](mailto:source@kello.ai) or use [Send a role](https://kello.ai/start).
</Tip>

## Who is Kello for?

- **Founders** hiring without a recruiter.
- **Recruiters and small talent teams** who need another sourcer on the team.
- **VC platform teams** hiring across a portfolio.

Kello takes any role in any function, entry level to C-suite, from Junior Accountant to Group CFO. It has sourced 4,700+ roles in 30+ countries, searching across 830M+ people.

## What are Kello and Kello Alpha?

There are two ways to use Kello. Pick the one that matches how much of the work you want to do yourself.

| | [Kello](/kello/quickstart) | [Kello Alpha](/alpha/overview) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Done-for-you sourcing service | Self-serve search app you chat with |
| **How you start** | Email a role to [source@kello.ai](mailto:source@kello.ai) | Sign in and describe who you want |
| **What you get** | Lists of up to 20 people, outreach, first conversations | Plain-language search over 830M+ professionals |
| **Who does the sourcing** | Kello | You |
