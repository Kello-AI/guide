Kello reaches out on your behalf to the candidates you approve: everyone you mark **Yes**, then everyone you mark **Maybe**, never anyone you mark **No**. A named person on the Kello team contacts them from their own LinkedIn, email and WhatsApp, checks your must-haves in the conversation, and brings interested people who fit to a first conversation with you.

## What is the goal of outreach?

To get interested candidates to a first-round conversation with you. The people on your list didn't apply, and most aren't actively looking. Outreach turns a strong profile into a real conversation.

## Who does Kello contact?

| You mark | Kello contacts them? |
| --- | --- |
| Yes | Yes, first |
| Maybe | Yes, after your Yes picks |
| No | Never |

Nobody is contacted until you say yes. If you mark more people Yes later on your list page, Kello contacts them too, with no need to ask again.

Already know someone, or they're in your pipeline? Tell Kello or mark them No, and they won't be contacted.

## How do I start outreach?

There are two ways:

- **Kello asks you.** Once you've marked candidates, Kello replies on your email thread offering to reach out, with a few quick questions.
- **You ask Kello.** Email Kello any time, for example: "Please start outreach for the people I marked Yes and Maybe." Kello replies with the same questions.

Either way, nobody is contacted until you've answered the required questions below.

## What does Kello ask?

Before Kello contacts anyone, it asks you a few quick things. One short reply covers it.

### Required

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| What should happen when a candidate is interested? | Share a calendar link, or name the person Kello should introduce candidates to by email. |
| What must every candidate meet? | Notice period, max salary, location, office days, visa. Kello checks these in the conversation and only brings you people who fit. |
| Anyone not to contact? | People you already know, people in your pipeline, or companies to keep out of it. |

### Optional

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Your pitch | The team, the manager, funding, the problems to solve. Kello builds these into each message. |
| Can the salary range be shared? | If yes, Kello shares it. If not, Kello asks each candidate's expectation and moves ahead if it fits your range. |
| Topics to avoid | Anything candidates shouldn't hear from Kello before they talk to you. |

## Example reply

Copy this, edit it, and send it as your reply.

```text
Calendar: https://cal.example.com/priya-30min - Priya (Engineering Manager) takes first calls.

Must-haves:
- Notice period 60 days or less
- Bangalore, 3 days a week in office
- Max 45 LPA fixed

Don't contact: anyone currently at our two main customers, and Rahul S. (already in our pipeline).

Pitch:
- Series A, raised last year, 30 people
- Small team, ships to enterprise customers every week
- Priya came from a large product company and runs a no-meetings-Wednesday team
- The hard problem: making our search fast at 10x today's data

Salary: OK to share 35-45 LPA fixed plus ESOPs.
Avoid: don't discuss the upcoming product launch.
```

A shorter reply works too. "Calendar link is X, notice under 60 days, Bangalore only, don't contact anyone at Y" is enough to start.

## Do I have to give a calendar link?

No. With a calendar link, each interested candidate books a first conversation directly with whoever takes it. Without one, Kello introduces the candidate by email to the person taking the hire forward. Kello connects candidates only with the person you name. See [first conversations](/kello/interviews-and-references).

## What makes a good pitch?

Specifics a candidate would care about. "Fast-growing startup" says little. "Series A, 30 people, you'd be the third engineer on the data team" gives Kello something to work with.

## Who sends the messages?

Our team. A named Kello person reaches out from their own LinkedIn, email and WhatsApp. Kello never uses your accounts.

A real person stands behind every conversation. AI gives them context on each candidate, so the message reads like it was written for them, because it was.

## Which channels does Kello use?

In India, most candidates reply on WhatsApp. Elsewhere, Kello reaches out on LinkedIn and email. Outreach works outside India too.

## What does the first message say?

It's about the role and why it fits this person. Kello reads each profile closely before anyone is contacted, so the message can speak to the candidate's actual work: what they built, the kind of team they've been on, why this next step makes sense for them. It also carries the pitch points you shared.

The message gets to the point. It doesn't open by asking about life goals.

## When does the candidate learn my company's name?

Only once they say they're open to a role like yours. Until then, the conversation is about the role itself. This keeps sensitive searches quiet, for example when you're replacing someone or setting up a new team before announcing it.

## What is the private role page?

Interested candidates get a private page with your full JD. They can read the role properly, at their own pace, and decide whether it's right for them. It's the same JD you sent Kello, shared only with candidates you approved.

## How does Kello check fit?

Your must-haves, such as notice period, salary, location, office days or visa, are checked during the conversation. Only people who fit are brought to you.

Candidates can ask questions. The Kello team member answers what they can from your brief and pitch. If a candidate asks something Kello can't answer, Kello checks with you before replying. Kello never makes up answers.

## Should I share the salary range?

It's your call. Sharing it lets candidates decide faster, and Kello shares it. Not sharing it is fine too: Kello asks each candidate's expectation and moves ahead only if it fits your range. Either way, you know what the candidate shared before the call.

## What happens when a candidate is interested?

If the candidate fits your must-haves, they're connected to the person you named:

- **With your calendar link:** the candidate books a first conversation directly with that person.
- **Without a calendar link:** Kello introduces the candidate by email to the person taking the hire forward.

See [first conversations, references and contact details](/kello/interviews-and-references).

## How do I get an update?

Reply to Kello and ask. "Where are we on outreach?" is enough. Ask whenever it's useful, for example before a hiring sync.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Booked time | The candidate is interested, fits your must-haves, and has booked a first conversation with the person you named. If you don't use a calendar link, Kello has introduced them by email instead. |
| Still talking | Kello is in conversation with the candidate. |
| Passed | The candidate isn't interested right now. You hear why. |

## Why do candidates say no?

You hear the reason, usually one of these:

- **Timing**: not the right moment to move.
- **Role**: the scope, level or kind of work isn't what they want.
- **Location**: the city or office days don't work.

These reasons sharpen your next list. If several people pass on the same thing, Kello takes that into account, and you may decide to adjust the role or the pitch.

Kello stays in touch with candidates who pass, in case they change their mind.

## Can I contact a candidate myself?

Yes. Ask Kello for their contact details.

Tell Kello when you reach out, so the candidate hears from one of you, not both. See [contact details](/kello/interviews-and-references).

## Can I change what candidates hear?

Anytime. Reply with the change: "mention the ESOPs", "ask about night shifts", "notice period can go to 90 days", "office days can be two, not three". Kello updates what candidates hear from then on.
