To give Kello feedback, mark each candidate **Yes**, **Maybe** or **No** on your list page and add a short note on why, or reply to Kello by email in plain words. Kello studies the people you said Yes and No to, reads your notes, and sends the next list of up to 20 new people, usually within a few hours. Each role includes up to 5 lists across 3 months from your first list.

## How do I mark candidates?

Open the link to your Kello list page. Each candidate has three options:

- **Yes**: you want to talk to this person.
- **Maybe**: worth a conversation, but not a first pick.
- **No**: not right for this role.

Next to each mark you can add a note on why. A few words are enough: "too senior", "love this one", "wrong kind of sales".

Anyone you share the list link with can review and mark candidates, so your hiring manager can give feedback directly.

## Do I need to add a note?

No, but notes help a lot. Kello studies the Yes and No profiles in depth to learn your team's taste. A mark tells Kello what you decided. A note tells Kello why, which makes the reason explicit instead of something Kello has to infer.

For example, two people marked No for different reasons look the same without a note. With "too senior" on one and "no pharma" on the other, Kello knows exactly what to change.

See the [feedback playbook](/kello/feedback-playbook) for what to write in common situations.

## Can I give feedback by email?

Yes. Reply to Kello's email in plain words. Some examples:

- "More B2B SaaS."
- "No pharma."
- "Must have managed 5+."
- "Bangalore only, no remote."

Email works well for feedback about the whole list rather than one person. You can mark candidates on the list page and send an email; Kello reads both.

## What about feedback after interviews?

Email Kello your interview feedback on candidates you've met. What you learn in a conversation is often the clearest signal of all: "strong on systems design, weak on customer calls" tells Kello something no profile can.

If your ATS is connected, Kello can pull interview feedback back from it. See [working with your team and ATS](/kello/team).

## What does Kello do with my feedback?

Kello uses your marks, notes and emails to understand what your team values, then searches again for your next list.

Feedback also decides who Kello reaches out to. Everyone you mark Yes is contacted first, then everyone you mark Maybe. Nobody you mark No is contacted. See [outreach](/kello/outreach).

<Tip>
Already know someone on the list, or they're in your pipeline? Mark them No, or tell Kello, and they won't be contacted.
</Tip>

## How do I get the next list?

Give feedback on the current one, on the list page, by email, or both. Kello uses it to search again and sends the next list, usually within a few hours.

## How many lists do I get?

| What you get | Per role |
| --- | --- |
| Lists | Up to 5 |
| People per list | Up to 20 |
| Time window | 3 months from your first list |
| Repeat candidates | None. Every list has only new people |

A list can have fewer than 20 people if not enough meet the bar. Kello tells you which requirement is narrowing the search so you can decide if it can bend. See [why a list has fewer than 20 people](/kello/your-list#why-are-there-fewer-than-20-people).

## Will I see the same people again?

No. Every new list contains only new people. You never see the same person twice for a role.

## How is the next list different?

It reflects what you told Kello. If you wrote "too senior" on three people and "love this one" on another, the next list leans toward what you liked.

If your feedback isn't moving the list the way you want, the [feedback playbook](/kello/feedback-playbook) has what to write for common cases.

## What if Kello misread the role?

Reply with the correction. "It's a Director role." "Pune, not Bangalore." Kello restarts on the corrected brief, even mid-search.

A correction is part of the same role, not a new one.

## What happens after 3 months?

If the role is still open after 3 months, you can renew it and Kello keeps going. See [kello.ai/pricing](https://kello.ai/pricing) for renewal terms.

## Is my feedback shared with anyone?

No. Your feedback is used only to get your own lists right. It shapes only your lists and is never used to train Kello's algorithms or shared with other customers. See [data, privacy and fairness](/kello/trust).
