Kello uses your job description, feedback and data only to personalise your own Kello experience, meaning to get your lists right.

- It never uses them to train its algorithms.
- It never shares them with other customers. Your JD is shared with candidates you approve. Otherwise, data is shared only with service providers that help run Kello, or where the law requires, as set out in the [privacy policy](https://kello.ai/privacy-policy).
- It never considers age, gender, religion, race, marital status or similar personal traits when it finds candidates.

## What does Kello do with my JD and feedback?

Kello uses them to understand your role and your team's taste, and to make your next list better. That's all.

| Your data | Used for | Not used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job description and brief | Searching for your role. Shown to candidates you approve, so they can review the role. | Training Kello's algorithms. Sharing with other customers. |
| Yes / Maybe / No marks and notes | Learning your team's taste for your next lists. | Shaping anyone else's lists. |
| Interview feedback | Sharpening your next lists. | Shaping anyone else's lists. |

## Is my data used to train Kello's AI?

No. Your JD, feedback and data are never used to train Kello's algorithms.

## Does my feedback affect other companies' lists?

No. Every search runs on its own. Your feedback shapes only your lists.

## Are my candidates shared with other companies?

Kello never pushes your candidates to anyone else.

## Who can see my list?

Only the people you share the list link with. Anyone with the link can open the list, review candidates and mark them. See [sharing a list with your team](/kello/team#how-do-i-share-a-list-with-my-team).

## Can I keep a search confidential?

When Kello reaches out, your company's name is revealed only once a candidate says they're open to a role like yours. This keeps sensitive searches quiet. Interested candidates then get a private page with your full JD. See [how outreach works](/kello/outreach#when-does-the-candidate-learn-my-companys-name).

## What does Kello never consider?

Age, gender, religion, race, marital status and similar personal traits. They play no part in who Kello looks for or who appears on your list.

## What if my brief asks for a personal trait?

Kello leaves it out and searches only on the job-related requirements in your brief. It doesn't swap a personal trait for a stand-in, such as turning an age into an experience cap. Describe what the role needs instead: skills, responsibilities and the kind of work the person has done.

The aim is to keep what you need, a level of experience or a kind of career, and drop what you shouldn't hire on. See [write a great brief](/kello/write-a-great-brief) for professional ways to say what you need.

## How does Kello approach diversity?

Kello doesn't search on personal traits, including for diversity. Instead, it looks across many kinds of companies, schools and career paths.

Kello also looks for several different kinds of people who could succeed in your role, so the list isn't one profile repeated 20 times.

## What does Kello look at instead?

Kello reads each profile the way a senior recruiter would:

- what each employer actually does
- the company's size and stage when the person joined and left
- whether they were promoted early
- what they worked on
- for senior roles, how their team or org performed

For engineering and AI roles, Kello can also look at public work such as code shipped or research published, only when the role calls for it.

<Note>
Missing accounts are never held against anyone.
</Note>

## Did these candidates apply?

No. Candidates on your list did not apply. Most aren't actively looking. Nobody is contacted until you mark them Yes or Maybe.

## Where does candidate data come from?

Kello works from public professional information. For details on how Kello handles personal data, including candidates' data, read the [Kello privacy policy](https://kello.ai/privacy-policy).

If you were contacted by Kello as a candidate, see [for candidates](/kello/for-candidates).
