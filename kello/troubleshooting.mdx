Most Kello problems are fixed with one short email reply: tell Kello what's wrong in plain words and it adjusts, even mid-search. Below are the common ones, each with the likely cause and the fix. For anything else, email **support@kello.ai**.

## I can't open the list link

**Cause:** Long links can break when they are copied, pasted or forwarded.

**Fix:** Click the link straight from Kello's email rather than copying it. If it still won't open, reply to that email or write to **support@kello.ai** and Kello will help.

## My colleague can't see the list

**Cause:** Your colleague doesn't have the list link. Only people you share the link with can see your results.

**Fix:** Forward them the link to your Kello list page. Anyone with the link can review and mark candidates. See [Use Kello with your team](/kello/team).

## The new list looks similar to the last one

**Cause:** Every new list contains only new people, so you won't see the same person twice for a role. If the profiles feel alike, Kello is still working from the same reading of your brief and feedback.

**Fix:** Tell Kello what to change, in plain words: "more B2B SaaS", "no pharma", "must have managed 5+". Add a note on why when you mark No. Notes like "too senior" make the reason explicit. See the [Feedback playbook](/kello/feedback-playbook).

## I got fewer than 20 people

**Cause:** Not enough people met the bar. Kello would rather send 14 strong people than pad the list.

**Fix:** Kello tells you which requirement is narrowing the search. Decide whether it can bend, and reply. See [Short or empty lists](/kello/your-list).

## I got no list at all

**Cause:** You never get an empty list. If nobody fits, Kello adjusts an assumption it made and searches again, or asks you one clear question about one of your requirements, such as "is the 8-year minimum firm?"

**Fix:** Answer the question with a one-line reply. If it's still stuck, someone from the Kello team steps in.

## Kello identified the wrong company

**Cause:** Your company name is shared with other companies, so Kello read the wrong one.

**Fix:** Reply with your company website. Include your website in future briefs too. Kello starts with your company, so getting it right matters.

## Kello misread the role

**Cause:** The JD left something open, or it was read in a way you didn't mean.

**Fix:** Reply with the correction: "it's a Director role", "Pune, not Bangalore". Kello restarts on the corrected brief, even mid-search. It's the same role, not a new one. Kello confirms how it read the role within minutes of your brief, so check that confirmation early.

## I haven't received my first list yet

**Cause:** The first list usually takes about two hours. If something important was missing from your brief, such as location or budget, Kello asks one short question before searching.

**Fix:** Check your inbox for a question from Kello and reply. A one-line answer is enough, for example "No fixed budget, use market rates." If there's no question and it's been more than a few hours, email **support@kello.ai**.

## No candidate has replied yet

**Cause:** Kello is reaching out, and replies take time. Most candidates aren't actively looking.

**Fix:** Ask Kello for an update anytime. You'll hear who has booked time, who's still talking, and who passed, with the reason. Marking more people Yes or Maybe widens who Kello contacts. See [Outreach status](/kello/outreach).

## Kello hasn't started outreach

**Cause:** Outreach starts when you reply to Kello's offer to reach out, or when you email Kello asking it to start. Before contacting anyone, Kello asks you a few quick questions, such as what should happen when a candidate is interested and which requirements every candidate must meet. Nobody is contacted until you mark Yes or Maybe.

**Fix:** Reply to Kello's questions, or email Kello asking it to start outreach for the role. One short reply is enough. See [Outreach setup](/kello/outreach).

## Someone on my list is already in my pipeline

**Cause:** Kello doesn't know who you've already spoken to.

**Fix:** Tell Kello and they won't be contacted, or mark them No.
