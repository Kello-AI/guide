To start with Kello, email your job description to **source@kello.ai** or use **Send a role** at [kello.ai/start](https://kello.ai/start). Kello sends your first list of up to 20 people in about two hours.

Kello (also called Kello Picks) is the sourcer on your team. You send the role. Kello finds the people, reaches out to the ones you approve, and sets up first conversations.

## How do I send my first role?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the JD">
    Email it to **source@kello.ai**, or use **Send a role** at [kello.ai/start](https://kello.ai/start). Both work the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what the JD doesn't say">
    Location, budget, what truly matters (must-haves vs nice-to-haves), and companies to hire from or avoid. A few lines is plenty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share people you'd hire in a heartbeat (optional)">
    Paste a few LinkedIn profiles. Kello studies what they have in common and finds more people like them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check how Kello read the role">
    Kello confirms its reading within minutes. If anything is off, reply with the correction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What can I send as a JD?

Anything you already have: a PDF, a Word doc, a link to the job post, or pasted text. No JD yet? Send the role name and a few lines on who you need.

If your company name is shared with other companies, include your website so Kello reads the right one.

## What does a first email look like?

```text
To: source@kello.ai
Subject: New role: Senior Backend Engineer

JD attached.

Location: Bangalore, 3 days a week in office
Budget: up to 45 LPA fixed
Must-haves: has run payments systems in production
Nice-to-have: fintech startup experience
Avoid: our two largest customers
People I'd hire in a heartbeat:
https://www.linkedin.com/in/...
https://www.linkedin.com/in/...
```

For ready-made emails by role, see [brief templates](/kello/brief-templates).

## What happens after I send it?

1. **Within minutes:** Kello confirms how it read the role. If something important is missing, like location or budget, Kello asks one short question first. A one-line reply is enough. "No fixed budget, use market rates" works.
2. **In about two hours:** your first list arrives as a link to your Kello list page, with up to 20 people.
3. **You mark each person** Yes, Maybe or No, with a note if you like.
4. **Kello reaches out** to the people you mark Yes, then Maybe, after a few quick questions about how you want it done.
5. **First conversations:** most roles have candidates ready to interview within 7 days.

Kello works weekends.

## Can I send more than one role?

Yes. Send **one role per email**. Each role gets its own search and its own list.

A role open in two cities is one role. List both locations in the same email.
