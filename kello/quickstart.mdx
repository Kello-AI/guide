To start with Kello, email your job description to [source@kello.ai](mailto:source@kello.ai). Kello sends your first list of up to 20 people in about two hours.

Kello (also called Kello Picks) is the sourcer on your team. You send the role. Kello finds the people, reaches out to the ones you approve, and sets up first conversations.

## How do I send my first role?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the JD">
    Email it from your work address to [source@kello.ai](mailto:source@kello.ai). Put the role and your company in the subject line.

    <Warning>Use your work email. Roles sent from personal addresses, such as Gmail, aren't accepted.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what the JD doesn't say">
    Location, budget, what truly matters (must-haves vs nice-to-haves), and companies to hire from or avoid. A few lines is plenty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share people you'd hire in a heartbeat">
    <Badge color="gray">Optional</Badge>

    Paste a few LinkedIn profiles. Kello studies what they have in common and finds more people like them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check how Kello read the role">
    Kello confirms its reading within minutes. If anything is off, reply with the correction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What does a first email look like?

```text Example first email wrap
To: source@kello.ai
Subject: Senior Backend Engineer – [Your company]

JD pasted below, PDF attached as backup.

Location: Bangalore, 3 days a week in office
Budget: up to 45 LPA fixed
Must-haves: has run payments systems in production
Nice-to-have: fintech startup experience
Avoid: our two largest customers
People I'd hire in a heartbeat:
https://www.linkedin.com/in/...
https://www.linkedin.com/in/...
```

LPA: lakhs per annum, yearly pay in Indian rupees (1 lakh = 100,000).

For ready-made emails by role, see [brief templates](/kello/brief-templates).

## What can I send as a JD?

Anything you already have: pasted text, a PDF, a Word doc or a link to the job post. Pasted text is the most reliable, so paste it where you can and attach the file as a backup. Some links, such as Notion pages, often can't be read. No <Tooltip tip="Job description: the document that describes the role.">JD</Tooltip> yet? Send the role name and a few lines on who you need, including what the person will do.

If your company name is shared with other companies, include your website so Kello reads the right one. See [which JD formats work best](/kello/write-a-great-brief#which-jd-formats-work-best).

## What happens after I send it?

1. **Within minutes:** Kello confirms how it read the role. If something important is missing, like location or budget, Kello asks one short question first. A one-line reply is enough. `No fixed budget, use market rates` works.
2. **In about two hours:** your first list arrives as a link to your Kello list page, with up to 20 people.
3. **When you're ready:** you mark each person Yes, Maybe or No, with a note if you like.
4. **After a few quick questions** about how you want it done: Kello reaches out to the people you mark Yes, then Maybe.
5. **Within 7 days for most roles:** first conversations, with candidates ready to interview.

<Info>Kello works weekends.</Info>

## Which email address should I use?

Your work email. Kello accepts roles from work email addresses only. Roles sent from personal addresses, such as Gmail, aren't accepted.

## What should the subject line say?

The role and your company. See [the subject line](/kello/write-a-great-brief#what-should-the-subject-line-say).

## Can Kello join a call?

No. Kello works over email and your list page. See [can Kello join a call?](/kello/team#can-kello-join-a-call)

## Can I send more than one role?

Yes. Send **one role per email**. Each role gets its own search and its own list.

A role open in two cities is one role. List both locations in the same email.
