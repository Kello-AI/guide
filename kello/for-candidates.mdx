Kello is a sourcing partner that hiring companies work with to find people for their open roles. If someone from the Kello team contacted you, it's because your public professional profile matched a role a company is hiring for, and they'd like to know if you're open to hearing more.

<Info>
**In short**

- You were contacted about one specific role that fits your experience.
- A named person from the Kello team reached out, on LinkedIn, email or WhatsApp.
- If you're interested, you get a private page with the full job description.
- You choose whether to be introduced to the company.
- Details you share, such as your notice period, expectations and CV, are passed to the person taking the hire forward.
- Not interested or bad timing? Just reply and say so.
- Questions? Reply to the person who contacted you, or email [support@kello.ai](mailto:support@kello.ai).
</Info>

## Who is Kello?

Kello is a sourcing partner based in Bengaluru. Companies send Kello their open roles, and Kello finds people whose experience fits and reaches out to see who's interested. Kello is run by XXV Century Private Limited and was founded in 2025. You can learn more at [kello.ai](https://kello.ai).

## Why was I contacted?

A company is hiring for a role, and your public professional profile suggests you could be a strong fit. Kello reads profiles the way a senior recruiter would: what you've worked on, the companies you've been part of and how your career has grown. The company reviewed your profile on a list Kello prepared and asked Kello to reach out.

You didn't need to apply, and you don't need to be actively looking. Most people Kello contacts aren't.

## Who will I be talking to?

A named person from the Kello team, from their own LinkedIn, email or WhatsApp. AI gives them context on the role and your background, so the message is about why this role fits you. A real person is on the other end of every conversation.

## Why don't I know the company's name yet?

Companies often keep their hiring quiet. Kello shares the company's name once you say you're open to a role like this. Until then, you'll hear about the role itself: what it is, the team and why it fits your background.

## What if the timing isn't right?

Just reply and say so. If you share why, such as timing, role or location, it helps shape the search for this role. Kello may stay in touch in case things change for you later.

## What happens if I'm interested?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the full job description">
    You get a private page with the company's full job description, so you can decide properly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your questions">
    Kello answers your questions. If Kello doesn't know an answer, it checks with the company before replying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share what matters for the role">
    Kello may ask a few things that matter for the role, such as your notice period, your salary expectations and when you'd be available. If the company has allowed it, Kello shares the salary range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on an introduction">
    You decide whether you'd like to be introduced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the hiring team">
    If you say yes, you book a first conversation directly on the hiring team's calendar, or Kello introduces you by email to the person taking the hire forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What does Kello share with the company?

When you're introduced, Kello shares what you've told it, such as your notice period, expectations and availability, and your CV if you've shared one.

<Note>
Kello connects you only with the person the company names for the role.
</Note>

## How does Kello handle my information?

Kello works from public professional information. Kello never considers personal traits such as age, gender, religion, race or marital status when deciding who might fit a role. You can read exactly how Kello handles personal data in the [privacy policy](https://kello.ai/privacy-policy).

## Who can I ask about this?

Reply to the person who contacted you, or email [support@kello.ai](mailto:support@kello.ai) with any question about Kello, the role or your information.
