Your whole hiring team can use Kello: CC your hiring manager on the role's email thread, share the link to your Kello list, and anyone with it can review candidates and mark them Yes, Maybe or No. Kello can also push candidates into your applicant tracking system (ATS) and pull interview feedback back, so your team keeps working where it already works.

## Should I CC the hiring manager?

Yes, from the first email if you can. Everyone on the thread gets access to the list, and Kello welcomes newcomers when they join. In Kello's words:

> "Access is refreshed automatically for everyone on the thread whenever you write to us."

The hiring manager can then correct the brief, mark candidates and reply with feedback directly, instead of passing it through you. It also means details like budget come from the person who owns them, not a guess.

## How do I share a list with my team?

Each list arrives as a link to your Kello list page. Forward that link to anyone involved in the hire: your hiring manager, a co-founder, a panel interviewer, an external recruiter you work with.

Anyone with the link can open the list, read each candidate's highlight, why this candidate, and <Tooltip tip="The one thing on each candidate Kello suggests you confirm." cta="See the list page" href="/kello/your-list#what-does-each-candidate-show">worth checking</Tooltip> note, and see how Kello approached the search.

<Warning>
Only people you share the link with see your results. Share it with the people who should weigh in, and no one else.
</Warning>

## Can everyone mark candidates?

Yes. Anyone with the link can mark candidates Yes, Maybe or No and add a note on why. Kello learns from all of it.

Notes make the reasons explicit. `Too senior for this team` or `love the startup background` tells Kello what your team values. When several people review, short notes also help your colleagues see each other's thinking.

## Can I download the list?

Yes. You can download the list from the list link. This helps if your team tracks candidates in a spreadsheet or wants to discuss them offline.

<Warning>
Files emailed back, such as a spreadsheet of your marks, aren't read. Mark on the list page or reply in words.
</Warning>

## How do I forward a list to a colleague?

Add them to the existing thread with Kello on CC. Don't start a new email or forward the list outside the thread. Kello can only reach people on the thread. When a colleague wasn't, Kello replied:

> "[Name] isn't currently on this thread's recipient list, so I can't notify her directly."

Kello reads the whole chain, including what you forward into it.

<Callout icon="quote-left" color="#9b5d98">
**Real example.** In one forwarded thread, a team member restated the location wrongly. Kello went back to the client's original email and corrected the city.
</Callout>

## Can colleagues email Kello too?

Yes. Anyone on the thread can reply in plain words. `More B2B SaaS` or `must have managed 5+` from a hiring manager is as useful as it is from you.

If two people disagree, settle it between you and send Kello one clear answer. Kello works best when the brief is consistent.

## Why one role per thread?

Kello keeps context per thread: the brief, your marks, your feedback and every list belong to that role. A message about a different role on the same thread confuses things. Kello will ask which role you mean.

<Warning>
Two <Tooltip tip="Job description: the document that describes the role.">JDs</Tooltip> pasted into one email can also mean the second one is missed.
</Warning>

Start a new email for each role, with the role and company in the subject line, for example:

```text Subject line wrap
SDE 2 – [Company]
```

See [what the subject line should say](/kello/write-a-great-brief#what-should-the-subject-line-say).

## Can Kello join a call?

No. Kello works over email and your list page, so it can't join a phone call or video meeting. The brief, corrections, written feedback and outreach answers go on the role's thread. Marks go on the list page.

For commercial questions, or to talk something through with a person, [talk to the Kello team](https://calendly.com/mona-gandhi-kello/ama).

## What does the ATS connection do?

If you use an ATS, Kello can connect to it. The connection works in two directions:

| Direction | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Kello to your ATS | Kello pushes candidates into the matching role in your ATS. |
| Your ATS to Kello | Kello pulls interview feedback back, and learns from it for your next lists. |

## Which ATS does Kello support?

Kello connects to seven ATS:

- Ashby
- Breezy HR
- Greenhouse
- Kula
- Lever
- Recruitee
- Workable

To connect yours, email [support@kello.ai](mailto:support@kello.ai) with the name of your ATS. If it's not on the list, [talk to a person](https://calendly.com/mona-gandhi-kello/ama).

## Why does interview feedback matter?

Kello learns from how your interviews go, not only from how you mark the list. If a candidate was strong on paper but missed in the interview, that tells Kello something your Yes mark couldn't.

Pulling feedback from your ATS means you don't have to write it twice. Your interviewers record it once, where they normally do.

## What if my ATS isn't supported?

Kello still works the same way. You can:

- Email interview feedback on candidates you've met to Kello. Plain words are fine.
- Download the list from the list link and add candidates to your ATS yourself.
- Share the list link with your team so everyone can review and mark candidates.

See [feedback and your next list](/kello/feedback#what-about-feedback-after-interviews) for what to send.
