Lean recruiting teams, such as a one- to three-person talent team at a growing tech company, use Kello as an extra sourcer: send each open role to source@kello.ai, get a list of up to 20 people with why each fits in about two hours, and Kello's team reaches out to the people you approve. Your recruiters keep the relationships, interviews and offers.

**TL;DR**

- Kello adds sourcing capacity without adding headcount or seats.
- One role per email; every role gets its own search and list.
- Outreach is done by a named Kello person from their own accounts, never yours.
- Kello can push candidates into your ATS and pull interview feedback back, for a select set of ATS.
- One flat fee per role: ₹75,000 in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes. First role free.

## Where do lean recruiting teams run out of time?

A small team usually spends most of its week on what can't be handed off: hiring manager syncs, interviews, scheduling, offers. Sourcing is what slips, especially for the hard roles. Kello takes that part.

## How does Kello fit into a small TA team?

Kello is the sourcer on your team. You stay the recruiter.

- **You send roles.** Email each one separately, with what the job description doesn't say: location, budget, must-haves, and companies to hire from or avoid.
- **Kello sends lists.** About two hours later, up to 20 people per role, each with a highlight, why they fit and one thing worth checking.
- **Your hiring managers review directly.** Share the list link. Anyone with it can review and mark Yes, Maybe or No.
- **Kello reaches out.** To everyone marked Yes, then Maybe. Never anyone marked No.
- **You take over at the first conversation.** Candidates book time with whoever you name, or Kello introduces them by email.

## Which roles should I send to Kello?

Send the roles your team doesn't have hours to source well:

- Hard-to-search roles, where the right people don't share a title.
- Roles outside your usual market, such as a new function or a new city or country.
- Urgent roles, where a list this week matters.
- Senior roles, where you want to see the market before choosing a path.

Kello covers any role and function, from Junior Accountant to Group CFO.

## Can Kello work alongside our agencies and job posts?

Yes. Kello doesn't ask for exclusivity. If someone is already in your pipeline, tell Kello or mark them No and they won't be contacted.

## Can my team reach candidates directly?

Yes. Ask for contact details for any candidate. They're included in the role fee, with no credits. Tell Kello when you reach out, so candidates hear from one of you, not both.

## Does Kello connect to our ATS?

Kello can push candidates into the role in your ATS and pull interview feedback back. A select set of ATS is supported. Email support@kello.ai to check yours. See [ATS](/kello/team).

## How do I keep track of outreach?

Ask anytime. Kello tells you who has booked time, who's still talking and who passed, with the reason. Reasons like timing, role or location sharpen the next list. See [Outreach status](/kello/outreach).

## How should a lean team budget for Kello?

Each role is one flat fee, ₹75,000 in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes, at any seniority. It covers 3 months: up to 5 lists, outreach, first conversations, contact details and reference-check guidance. There are no seats, credits, subscription or minimum.
