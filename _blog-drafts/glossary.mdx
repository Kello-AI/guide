This glossary defines the recruiting and AI sourcing terms used across the Kello guide, in plain language, in alphabetical order. Each entry starts with a one-sentence definition that stands on its own.

**TL;DR**

- Market terms such as contingency fee, retained search and RPO describe how recruiting is bought.
- India terms such as CTC, LPA and notice period come up in almost every Indian hire.
- Kello terms such as list, round and Yes / Maybe / No describe how working with Kello goes.

## AI recruiter

An AI recruiter is a product or service that uses AI to do recruiting work, most often sourcing candidates and reaching out to them. The term is broad: it can mean a self-serve tool your team operates or a done-for-you service. See [How to choose an AI recruiter](/learn/how-to-choose-an-ai-recruiter).

## AI sourcing agent

An AI sourcing agent is a service that takes a job brief and returns a list of people who fit the role, with the reasons they fit, instead of giving you a search tool to run yourself. Kello is an AI sourcing agent. See [What is an AI sourcing agent?](/learn/what-is-an-ai-sourcing-agent)

## Brief

A brief is everything you tell a sourcer about a role: the job description plus what it doesn't say, such as location, budget, must-haves and companies to hire from or avoid. See [Write a great brief](/kello/write-a-great-brief).

## Calibration

Calibration is the process of aligning a sourcer's idea of a good candidate with yours, usually through example profiles and feedback on early candidates. With Kello, sharing profiles of people you'd hire and marking Yes or No with a note are how calibration happens.

## Contingency fee

A contingency fee is a recruitment agency fee paid only when you hire a candidate the agency introduced, typically around 15–25% of first-year salary. See [Recruiting pricing models](/learn/recruiting-pricing-models).

## CTC

CTC (cost to company) is the total annual amount an employer spends on an employee in India, usually including fixed pay, variable pay and employer contributions such as provident fund. Take-home pay is lower than CTC. See [Hiring in India](/learn/hiring-in-india).

## First-round conversation

A first-round conversation is the first call between a hiring team and an interested candidate. With Kello, candidates book it directly on your calendar, or Kello introduces them by email to the person you name.

## Flat fee per role

A flat fee per role is a fixed price for sourcing one specific opening, the same regardless of seniority or salary. Kello charges ₹75,000 per role in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes.

## Forward deployed engineer (FDE)

A forward deployed engineer is a customer-facing software engineer who embeds with customers to deploy, integrate and adapt a product to their systems. See [How to hire a forward deployed engineer](/learn/hiring-forward-deployed-engineers).

## Job description (JD)

A job description is the written summary of a role: responsibilities, requirements and often location and pay. Kello accepts a JD as a PDF, Word doc, link or pasted text, or just a role name and a few lines on who you need.

## Kello

Kello (also called Kello Picks) is an AI sourcing agent: you send a job description and get a list of up to 20 people with why each fits, and a named Kello person reaches out to the people you approve. See [Quickstart](/kello/quickstart).

## Kello Alpha

Kello Alpha is Kello's self-serve search app, where you search professionals and companies in plain language. See [Kello Alpha](/alpha/overview).

## List

A list is the set of people Kello recommends for a role, up to 20 per list, delivered as a link to your list page. Other services may call this a shortlist. See [Your list](/kello/your-list).

## LPA

LPA means lakhs per annum, the usual way annual pay is quoted in India, where one lakh is ₹1,00,000. A salary of 25 LPA is ₹25,00,000 a year.

## Must-have

A must-have is a requirement a candidate has to meet to be considered, as opposed to a nice-to-have. Kello checks your must-haves, such as notice period, maximum salary or location, during conversations with candidates.

## Notice period

A notice period is the time an employee must work after resigning before they can leave, often 30–90 days in India. It shapes when a new hire can start.

## Outbound recruiting

Outbound recruiting means reaching out to people who haven't applied, instead of waiting for applicants. Most people Kello finds aren't actively looking.

## Outreach

Outreach is the first contact with a candidate about a role. Kello's outreach is done by a named Kello person from their own LinkedIn, email and WhatsApp, to people you mark Yes, then Maybe. See [Outreach](/kello/outreach).

## Passive candidate

A passive candidate is someone who isn't actively looking for a job but may be open to the right role. Most people on a Kello list are passive candidates.

## Recruitment process outsourcing (RPO)

RPO is an arrangement where an outside firm runs part or all of a company's recruiting, often priced as a monthly fee over a contract of several months or more.

## Reference check

A reference check is a conversation with someone who has worked with a candidate, to confirm what you've learned. Kello tells you who to speak to and what to ask, and shares their contact details; you make the calls. See [Reference checks](/kello/interviews-and-references).

## Retained search

Retained search is a recruiting model, mostly for senior roles, where a search firm is paid in stages, often around 25–35% of first-year salary, usually on an exclusive basis.

## Round

A round is one list of up to 20 people for a role, plus your feedback on it. Each Kello role includes up to 5 rounds over 3 months, each with only new people. See [Next lists](/kello/feedback).

## Shortlist

A shortlist is a small set of candidates selected for a closer look. In Kello, this is called your [list](/kello/your-list).

## Sourcing

Sourcing is finding people who could fit a role and deciding who is worth contacting, before any interviews happen.

## Time to first list

Time to first list is how long it takes from sending a role to receiving the first set of candidates. With Kello, it's about two hours.

## Time to interview

Time to interview is how long it takes from sending a role to having candidates ready for a first conversation. Most Kello roles have candidates ready to interview within 7 days.

## Warm intro

A warm intro is an introduction to a candidate made by someone they already know or have spoken to, instead of a cold message. When a candidate is interested and you have no calendar link, Kello introduces them by email to the person taking the hire forward.

## Yes / Maybe / No

Yes, Maybe and No are the marks you give each person on your Kello list. Kello reaches out to Yes first, then Maybe, never No, and learns from all three. See [Feedback](/kello/feedback).
