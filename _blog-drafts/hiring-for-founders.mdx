Interested candidates book a first conversation on your calendar, or Kello introduces them by email. The first role is free, one per company.

- Your time goes into deciding and interviewing, not searching and messaging.

- Your company name is revealed only once a candidate is open to a role like yours, which keeps sensitive searches quiet.
- Most roles have candidates ready to interview within 7 days.
- After the free role: ₹75,000 per role in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes. No subscription.

## Why is hiring hard for founders?

Early hires matter most, and founders have the least time for them. Job posts bring applicants, but rarely the people you'd most want, who usually aren't looking. Finding those people means hours of searching, reading profiles and writing messages, alongside building the product and selling it.

## How does Kello help a founder hire?

Kello takes on the sourcing and the first outreach. You stay in charge of who you meet and who you hire.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the role">
    Email **source@kello.ai** or use **Send a role** at [kello.ai/start](https://kello.ai/start). PDF, doc, link or a few pasted lines all work. Include your website if your company name is shared with others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check how Kello read it">
    Within minutes Kello confirms how it read the role. If it's off, reply with the correction in one line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your list">
    About two hours later, open your list: up to 20 people, each with a highlight, why they fit your role and one thing worth checking. Mark Yes, Maybe or No.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Kello reach out">
    Answer a few quick questions, such as your calendar link and any must-haves. A named Kello person then contacts your Yes and Maybe picks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet interested people">
    Candidates who are interested and meet your must-haves book time on your calendar, or Kello introduces them by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What should a founder put in the brief?

Say what the job description doesn't:

- **What truly matters.** The two or three things that would make you hire someone, separate from the nice-to-haves.
- **Location and budget.** "No fixed budget, use market rates" works if you're unsure.
- **Companies to hire from or avoid.**
- **People you'd hire in a heartbeat.** Share their LinkedIn profiles. Kello studies what they have in common and finds more people like them.

See [Write a great brief](/kello/write-a-great-brief) and copy-paste [brief templates](/kello/brief-templates), including one for a Founding Engineer.

## What does Kello know about my company?

Kello starts with your company: what it does, how it has grown, and the kind of people who have joined. That context shapes who fits. It also weighs whether a candidate would say yes. Someone for whom your role is a step down in title, scope or company is left out.

## How do I pitch a role when nobody knows my startup?

Before outreach, Kello asks for pitch points: your team, the person they'd work with, funding, and the problems they'd solve. A named Kello person builds each message around the role and why it fits that candidate. Interested candidates get a private page with your full job description. You can change what candidates hear anytime: "mention the ESOPs".

## Can my co-founder review the list too?

Yes. Share the list link with your co-founder or anyone involved in the hire. Anyone with the link can review and mark candidates. There's no per-seat pricing.
