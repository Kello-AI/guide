VC platform and talent teams use Kello to give portfolio companies a sourcer on demand: each company sends a role to source@kello.ai and gets a list of up to 20 people with why each fits in about two hours, then a named Kello person reaches out to the people the company approves. Every company gets its first role free.

**TL;DR**

- Covers any role and function across your portfolio, from a first engineer to a CFO.
- Each role gets its own search. A company's feedback shapes only that company's lists.
- Every portfolio company gets its first role free, one per company.
- Flat fee per role after that: ₹75,000 in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes. No subscription or minimum.
- Kello also has a self-serve search app, [Kello Alpha](/alpha/overview), for founder and talent research.

## What does a VC platform team need from a sourcing partner?

A platform team supports many companies at different stages, often with a small team of its own. Requests are uneven: a seed company's first engineer one week, a growth company's finance leader the next. What helps is something founders can use directly, that works for any role, costs a known amount, and doesn't need the platform team in every step.

## How does Kello work across a portfolio?

- **Founders send roles directly.** Email **source@kello.ai** or use **Send a role** at [kello.ai/start](https://kello.ai/start). One role per email.
- **Each company gets its own list.** Up to 20 people per list, each with a highlight, why they fit that company's role and one thing worth checking.
- **Kello learns each company's taste.** It starts with what the company does and the kind of people it has hired, then learns from the founder's Yes and No marks.
- **Kello reaches out.** A named Kello person contacts the people the company approves, from their own LinkedIn, email and WhatsApp, and sets up first conversations with the person the company names.

Most roles have candidates ready to interview within 7 days.

## Who should send the role: the platform team or the founder?

Usually the founder or the person hiring. Kello's questions, the list and the outreach setup all go to whoever sends the role, and the decisions sit with them. If you're making the introduction, copy the founder on the first email so they can take it from there. The list link can be shared, so a platform lead can review alongside the founder.

## Is each company's search kept separate?

Yes. Every search runs on its own, and a company's feedback shapes only its own lists. A company's job description, feedback and data are used only to get its own lists right and are never shared with other customers. Kello never pushes one company's candidates to anyone else. In the rare case Kello recommends one of a company's candidates for another company's role, Kello tells them.

## Can Kello help with confidential searches?

Yes. A company's name is revealed to a candidate only once that candidate says they're open to a role like theirs. That keeps searches such as a leadership replacement quiet.

## How does pricing work for portfolio companies?

Each company's first role is free: no card, no contract and no invoice, even if they hire. After that, one flat fee per role, ₹75,000 in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes, at any seniority. Each company keeps everyone Kello finds and their contact details, with no placement fee. To discuss working with Kello across your portfolio, [talk to the Kello team](https://calendly.com/mona-gandhi-kello/ama).

## What about founder and talent research?

For research rather than filling a specific role, such as founders to watch, people leaving a company, or who worked with whom, see [Kello Alpha](/alpha/overview) and its guide to [founders and talent moves](/alpha/playbooks).
