An AI sourcing agent like Kello finds and reads candidates for you in hours and charges a flat fee per role, while a recruitment agency uses its own recruiters and network and typically charges a percentage of the hire's first-year salary. Agencies are often the better choice for a few highly specialised or confidential executive searches; an AI sourcing agent is usually faster and more predictable for most other roles.

Last reviewed: October 2026

**TL;DR**

- Contingency agencies typically charge around 15–25% of first-year salary, paid on hire. Retained search often runs around 25–35%, paid in stages.
- Kello charges one flat fee per role: ₹75,000 in India or US$3,000 elsewhere, excluding taxes. First role free.
- Kello's first list arrives in about two hours, with up to 20 people and the reasons each one fits.
- Kello doesn't ask for exclusivity, and there's no placement fee if you hire someone it found later.
- You can use both: Kello for breadth and speed, an agency for a handful of hard senior searches.

## What's the real difference?

**Breadth versus depth of hand-holding.** An agency typically presents a few candidates it has already spoken to. Kello shows you up to 20 people it has read closely, you decide who's worth a conversation, and then Kello reaches out. You see more of the market, and you choose earlier.

**How the fee behaves.** An agency fee rises with the salary you pay. Kello's fee is the same for a Junior Accountant and a Group CFO, so it never has a reason to push a more expensive candidate. You know the cost on the day you send the role.

**When you pay.** A contingency agency usually bills only on a hire. Kello bills per role, when your first list arrives, so you see the list before you pay. If Kello finds nobody, there's no charge. If you said Yes to people and Kello couldn't connect you with even one candidate, the fee is returned.

**What you can see.** Kello shows how it read your role, what it learned from your past hires, the kinds of people it looked for and what it relaxed. If the reasoning is off, you correct it in one line and Kello searches again.

## When is a recruitment agency the better choice?

An agency is often the better fit when:

- **The search is a small, senior, relationship-driven hire** where a recruiter's long-standing personal network matters more than breadth, such as a board seat or a sensitive leadership replacement.
- **You want someone else to run the whole process**, including multiple interview rounds, offer negotiation and closing.
- **You're hiring in a niche where an agency has years of relationships** with a small, known pool of people.
- **You have no time to review a list.** Kello works best when someone marks Yes, Maybe or No and says why.

None of these exclude Kello. You can run an agency on one hard search and Kello on everything else, or both on the same role.

## When is an AI sourcing agent the better choice?

- You want candidates this week, not next month.
- You want a predictable cost per role regardless of salary.
- You're hiring several different roles and need breadth on each.
- You want to see who is out there and why, not only the few people someone else chose.
- You want to keep every candidate's details with no placement fee later.

## FAQ

### How does the cost compare with a recruitment agency?

They're priced differently. An agency fee rises with the salary: on a US$100,000 hire, a 20% fee is US$20,000. Kello's fee is US$3,000 (₹75,000 in India) whatever the salary, fixed before the search starts, so Kello never has a reason to push a more expensive candidate.

### Can I use Kello and an agency on the same role?

Yes. Kello doesn't ask for exclusivity. If someone is already in your pipeline from another source, tell Kello and they won't be contacted.

### Does Kello screen candidates like an agency does?

Kello checks your must-haves, such as notice period, salary and location, during its conversations with candidates, and only brings you people who fit. Interviews and offers stay with you.

### Who owns the candidates Kello finds?

You do. You keep everyone Kello finds and their contact details, and there's no placement fee, even if you hire someone months later.

### What if Kello can't find anyone?

There's no charge if Kello finds nobody. If a list is short, Kello tells you which requirement is narrowing the search so you can decide whether it can bend.
