## What is CTC?

CTC, or cost to company, is the total annual amount a company spends on an employee. It usually includes fixed salary and variable pay or bonus, and often employer contributions such as provident fund, gratuity and some benefits. Because of these components, monthly take-home pay is lower than CTC divided by 12. Offers and salary discussions in India are almost always framed in CTC.

When you set a budget, say whether it's total CTC or fixed pay, and how much is variable.

## What does LPA mean?

LPA means lakhs per annum. One lakh is 100,000 rupees, written ₹1,00,000 in the Indian numbering system. A salary of "25 LPA" means a CTC of ₹25,00,000 a year. A hundred lakh make one crore (₹1,00,00,000), so senior pay is often quoted in crores.

## How do notice periods work in India?

Most employees must give notice before leaving, and in India it's often 30–90 days, with longer periods common at larger companies and in senior roles. Some employers allow a buyout, where the new employer or the candidate pays to shorten the notice period. Candidates often state whether they're serving notice already and how long is left.

For hiring, a long notice period can matter as much as fit. If you need someone within a set time, make the maximum notice period a must-have.

## What should an India brief include?

Add these to the job description when you send a role to Kello:

- **Budget as a CTC range in LPA**, and whether it includes variable pay. "No fixed budget, use market rates" works if you're unsure.
- **Maximum notice period**, if it's firm.
- **City and office days.** A role open in Bengaluru and Pune is one role; list both locations.
- **Companies to hire from or avoid.**
- **Must-haves vs nice-to-haves.**

Before outreach, Kello asks for the requirements every candidate must meet, such as notice period, maximum salary, location and office days, and checks them during conversations. Only people who fit are brought to you.

## How does Kello reach candidates in India?

A named person from the Kello team reaches out from their own LinkedIn, email and WhatsApp, never your accounts. In India, most candidates reply on WhatsApp, so it's part of how Kello reaches people. Messages are built around your role and why it fits each candidate.

Your company's name is revealed only once a candidate says they're open to a role like yours. If you allow it, Kello shares the salary range; otherwise it asks the candidate's expectation and moves ahead if it fits your range.

Before a first conversation, you know what the candidate shared with Kello, which can include notice period, current and expected CTC, availability and their CV. Not every candidate shares every detail.
