Reaching out to candidates one by one is slow, repetitive work. When you review a Kello shortlist, mark the candidates you want as **Yes**, and ask us to reach out on LinkedIn, this extension is what carries that out: it sends the connection requests and follow-ups from **your own LinkedIn account**, at a safe, human pace, while you get on with everything else.

You approve who to contact in Kello. Kello handles the messages and timing — the extension does the clicking.

## What it does for you

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sends connection requests" icon="user-plus">
    Works through the candidates you marked **Yes** on your shortlist and sends invites for you — no copy-pasting, no manual clicking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follows up automatically" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Once someone accepts, Kello sends the opening message in your voice, then keeps the conversation moving with the right timing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Replies in your voice" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    When a candidate replies, Kello drafts a helpful, natural response — and hands the thread back to you at the moment a human should take over.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paces itself safely" icon="shield-check">
    A small number of invites per day, spaced several minutes apart, only during working hours — the way a careful person would do it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Everything shows up on the extension's own **Outreach dashboard**, opened right from its popup: who's been invited, who connected, who replied, and which conversations are waiting for you.

<Info>
  **Your account stays yours.** Kello never sees or stores your LinkedIn password or login. Everything happens right in your own browser, on the session you're already signed into. See [Is this safe?](#is-this-safe) below.
</Info>

## Before you start

You'll need three things — all of which you probably already have:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Google Chrome">
    The extension runs on Chrome. If you use another browser day-to-day, that's fine — just use Chrome for this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signed in to Kello">
    You have a Kello account and can log in at [kello.ai/app](https://kello.ai/app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signed in to LinkedIn">
    You're logged into LinkedIn in that same Chrome browser, as you normally would be.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep Kello and LinkedIn logged in **in the same Chrome window** you use every day. The extension works quietly in the background there.
</Tip>

## Download and install

This one isn't in the Chrome Web Store (LinkedIn tools generally can't be), so installing takes a few extra clicks the first time. Follow along — it's about two minutes, and you only do it once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the extension">
    Click this link to download the file:

    [**⬇️ Download Kello Outreach**](https://github.com/Kello-AI/kello-outreach-releases/releases/latest/download/kello-outreach.zip)

    It saves a file called `kello-outreach.zip` (usually to your **Downloads** folder).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Unzip it — and keep the folder">
    Open the file to unzip it. This creates a **folder** called `kello-outreach`.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Windows">
        Right-click `kello-outreach.zip` → **Extract All…** → **Extract**. Move the resulting `kello-outreach` folder somewhere you won't touch it — for example your **Documents** folder.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Mac">
        Double-click `kello-outreach.zip`. A `kello-outreach` folder appears next to it. Move it somewhere safe — for example your **Documents** folder.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Warning>
      Don't delete or move this folder after you've installed it. Chrome reads the extension from this exact location — if it disappears, the extension stops working. Pick a permanent home for it now.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Chrome's extensions page">
    In Chrome, click the address bar, type this, and press Enter:

    ```
    chrome://extensions
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on Developer mode">
    Find the **Developer mode** switch in the **top-right corner** of that page and turn it **on**. A new row of buttons appears.

    <Note>
      "Developer mode" sounds technical, but it just lets Chrome run an extension you downloaded directly (rather than from the store). It's safe to leave on.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Load the extension">
    Click **Load unpacked** (top-left). In the window that opens, select the **`kello-outreach` folder** you unzipped, and confirm.

    You'll see **Kello Outreach** appear in your list of extensions. 🎉
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pin it so you can find it">
    Click the little **puzzle-piece icon** near the top-right of Chrome, then click the **pin** next to **Kello Outreach**. Its icon now sits in your toolbar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link your Kello account

One last step connects the extension to your Kello workspace — no password needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make sure you're signed in to Kello">
    Open [kello.ai/app](https://kello.ai/app) in the same browser and log in if you aren't already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Kello Outreach icon, then “Link your Kello account”">
    The extension opens Kello briefly, links itself to your signed-in session, and the status flips to **Kello · linked**. That's it — you're connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You're all set. Outreach now runs on its own during your working day. There's nothing to click each morning — just keep Chrome open.
</Check>

## What you'll see

Click the extension icon any time to check in. The popup shows:

- **Kello · linked** and **LinkedIn · ready** — the two green lights that mean everything's connected.
- **A running summary** — how many candidates were invited, connected, messaged, and replied.
- **Last run** — when it last did some work.
- **Open dashboard** — opens the extension's full Outreach view, with each job's funnel and any conversations waiting on you.

## Staying in control

You're always the boss. The extension is built to be cautious and to step aside the moment you want to take over.

- **It only works during your working day** (roughly 8am–8pm, your local time) — never overnight.
- **It only runs while Chrome is open.** Close your browser and it pauses; open it again and it picks back up.
- **It paces itself** — a safe daily limit of invites, spaced several minutes apart, so your outreach looks natural.
- **If you jump into a conversation yourself**, Kello notices and automatically hands that thread back to you — it won't talk over you.
- **Pause any time** from the extension's Outreach dashboard.
- **If LinkedIn ever asks you to verify it's you**, the extension quietly pauses and waits — just complete LinkedIn's prompt and it resumes on its own.

## Keeping it up to date

Because this extension isn't installed from the Chrome Web Store, it doesn't update itself automatically. It'll tell you when there's a new version:

- **"Update available"** — you'll see a gentle banner in the popup. When it's convenient, [download the latest version](https://github.com/Kello-AI/kello-outreach-releases/releases/latest/download/kello-outreach.zip) and repeat the unzip + **Load unpacked** steps above (it replaces the old one).
- **"No longer supported"** — occasionally a version gets too old to run safely, and outreach will **stop** until you update. If you ever see this, just grab the latest version the same way and you're back in business.

<Tip>
  Updating is exactly the same as installing — download, unzip, **Load unpacked**. If you kept your folder in a permanent spot, you can also just replace its contents with the new files and click the **refresh icon** on the extension's card in `chrome://extensions`.
</Tip>

## Is this safe?

Short answer: yes, and it's designed to keep *your account* safe too.

- **We never have your LinkedIn password.** Kello can't log into LinkedIn — the extension simply acts inside the browser session you're already signed into.
- **Nothing runs on our servers touching LinkedIn.** All the LinkedIn activity happens in *your* browser, on *your* computer.
- **It behaves like a careful human** — modest daily volumes, natural gaps between actions, working hours only. No blasting.
- **It backs off automatically** if LinkedIn shows any verification prompt, so it never pushes when it shouldn't.
- **You can stop it instantly** — pause in Kello, or remove the extension from `chrome://extensions` and all activity ends immediately.

For the full picture on how Kello handles your data, see our [Data & Privacy](https://kello.ai/privacy-policy) page.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to leave my computer on all day?">
    No — you just need **Chrome open** while you're working. When your computer is off or Chrome is closed, outreach simply pauses and resumes next time you're online, during working hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will it message people without me knowing?">
    It only works through the candidates you approved on your shortlist, and everything it does is visible on the extension's Outreach dashboard. The moment a conversation needs a human touch, it hands the thread to you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will it discuss salary or budget with candidates?">
    No. Kello never shares the role's pay or budget with a candidate on your behalf. If a candidate raises compensation, Kello finds out what *they're* looking for and flags the conversation for you — it never commits to a number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I reply to a candidate myself?">
    Kello notices and steps back from that conversation automatically, marking it as yours to handle. It won't send AI replies on top of your messages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't it in the Chrome Web Store?">
    LinkedIn-automation tools generally aren't allowed in the store, so we distribute it directly. That's why the first install needs "Developer mode" and "Load unpacked" — after that, it just works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I pause everything?">
    Open the extension's Outreach dashboard and pause the campaign. To stop completely, remove the extension from `chrome://extensions` — all activity ends right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see “update available” — what do I do?">
    When it's convenient, download the latest version and repeat the unzip + **Load unpacked** steps. Your setup and history stay exactly as they were.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use this on a different computer?">
    Yes — just install it on that computer too and link your Kello account the same way. Make sure you're logged into LinkedIn there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
