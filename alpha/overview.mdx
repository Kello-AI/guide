Kello Alpha is an AI recruiting copilot that answers "who should I hire, invite, back or call?" in plain English. Sign in at [kello.ai/login](https://kello.ai/login), describe the person, paste a job description or drop in LinkedIn profiles, and Kello Alpha searches 830M+ professionals in 200+ countries and 57M companies, checks its own results, and gives you a saved list you can review, refine and export.

## What does Kello Alpha do?

Kello Alpha turns a sentence into a search. There are no boolean strings, filter panels or dropdowns.

- **Finds people** by role, seniority, companies, schools, location, years of experience and career shape: "joined when the company had under 100 people", "promoted twice at the same company", "left Google for a startup".
- **Hires from a job description.** Paste a JD or a link. Alpha shows how it read the role before it searches.
- **Learns from examples.** Paste up to 10 LinkedIn profiles of people you'd hire. Alpha works out what they share.
- **Runs built-in playbooks** for reference checks, event guest lists, founders to watch, ex-founders and acqui-hires, career moves, and who worked with whom.
- **Answers market questions** about companies, talent flow, headcount trends and live job postings, with real numbers and charts.
- **Tells you what it did.** A second AI reviews the top results of every people search. If Alpha loosened a must-have or couldn't filter on something, it says so.

## Who is Kello Alpha for?

Kello Alpha reads your title and company and adjusts to who is asking. An agency recruiter, an in-house recruiter and an investor get different defaults.

| You are | You use Alpha for |
|---|---|
| Executive search or consulting firm | Senior and niche mandates, mapping a firm's team, business development lists |
| VC, scout or platform team | Founders to watch, ex-founders, early employees at breakout companies, LP and family-office lists |
| In-house recruiter or talent partner | Hard-to-fill roles, sourcing from a JD, mapping competitors |
| Founder hiring the early team | First engineers, first sales hire, a founding PM |
| Hiring manager | Calibrating "people like this", checking a shortlist |
| Community or events lead | Invite lists for meetups, dinners and conferences |

## Kello Alpha vs Kello (done-for-you)

Kello has two ways to find people. Kello Alpha is the one you drive. Kello is the done-for-you sourcing service.

| | Kello Alpha | Kello (done-for-you) |
|---|---|---|
| Who does the work | You search, interactively, in chat | Kello's team and AI do it for you |
| How you start | Sign in and type a question | Email a role to source@kello.ai or use kello.ai/start |
| What you get | Lists of up to 1,000 people, refined as you chat | Up to 20 people per list, each with reasons they fit your role |
| Outreach | None. You reach people through your own tools | Our team reaches out to the people you approve |
| Contact details | Not provided | Available for any candidate you want to reach |
| Best for | Mapping, research, exploring a market, many quick questions | One open role you want filled with interested candidates |

Use Alpha when you want to explore. Send a role to Kello when you want people ready to talk. See the [Kello quickstart](/kello/quickstart).

## How big is the data behind Kello Alpha?

- 830M+ professional profiles in 200+ countries, with deep coverage in the US and India
- 57M companies and institutions
- Millions of live job postings

The data is richest on work history: dates, titles, seniority, tenure, and the company's stage and size when someone was there.

## How do I sign in?

Go to [kello.ai/login](https://kello.ai/login). Alpha is the default page after you sign in, so you land straight in the chat. On the welcome screen you'll see "Meet Alpha." and a box to type in.

## What should I type first?

Describe the person the way you'd describe them to a colleague. One or two sentences is enough.

```text
Senior backend engineers in Pune who've worked at a product company
```

```text
People who joined Razorpay when it had fewer than 100 employees
```

You can also paste a job description, a link to one, or LinkedIn profile links of people you'd hire. See [finding people](/alpha/finding-people) for what makes a good ask.

## What happens while Alpha works?

You see progress live:

- **A short plan** with checkmarks as each step finishes.
- **Step labels** such as "Searching candidates…".
- **The narrowing funnel**, showing how the pool shrinks as Alpha applies your criteria.
- **A "Thought for Ns" panel** you can expand to see Alpha's reasoning.

Alpha searches, then has a second AI review the top results against the person you described. If people who don't fit leaked in, or good people were missed, Alpha fixes the search (at most twice) before showing you anything.

You can close the tab. Alpha keeps working, and the answer is there when you come back. You can also press stop at any time; the partial answer is kept.

## Will Alpha ask me questions?

At most one, and only when a wrong guess would waste the search. For example, "which Apple?" You'll see a **Before I search** card with choices or a text box. Answer it and Alpha carries on.

## Where does Alpha search by default?

In your home country. Alpha says so in its answer, for example "Searched in India". To widen it, reply "search globally".

## How do I read the list card?

Every people search ends with a list card:

- **"N matches found"** with the top 4 people shown.
- A one-sentence description of exactly who the list contains, plus any assumptions Alpha made.
- **View N candidates** opens the full results page.

Alpha also writes a short answer above the card. If it loosened a must-have or couldn't filter on something, it says so there in plain words.

A list holds up to 1,000 people, shown 24 per page. Use the page controls at the bottom of the results page to see the rest.

<Tip>
Lists are frozen when first opened, so the same list shows the same order every time you or a colleague come back to it.
</Tip>

## What can I do next?

- Open a profile to see why the person matched. See [working with results](/alpha/working-with-results).
- Refine in chat: "limit it to Bengaluru", "only people at Series B companies", "exclude agencies".
- Ask "export this as CSV". See [working with results](/alpha/working-with-results).

## Can I edit or redo a message?

Yes. Edit any message (the old version is kept), retry an answer, and flip between versions with the arrows. Copy any answer as Markdown. Start fresh with **New Search**. Your conversations are grouped by day in the history.

Alpha works on mobile too. History opens from a drawer. The chat rail next to the results page is desktop only.
