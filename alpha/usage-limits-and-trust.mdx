Kello Alpha has a usage budget per user per cycle, measured by the AI work it does for you, not a count of searches. Kello Alpha doesn't provide emails or phone numbers, benchmark salaries, take file uploads, search your ATS or send outreach, and it makes eight promises the product enforces: it never invents facts, says what it changed, checks its own work, respects your examples, refuses discriminatory searches, exposes no contact data, never acts in your apps without your click, and stays on task.

## What uses my budget?

The work Alpha does on your messages: reading what you sent, searching, checking its results and writing the answer. It's not a fixed price per search. A simple lookup uses less than a large search with several rounds of checking, or a playbook such as a reference check.

## Where do I see my usage?

Go to **My Account** and open **Usage** (shown as **Plan and usage** on some accounts). You'll see your search budget as a percentage remaining.

## Will I be warned before I run out?

Yes. Kello emails you when you've used 50%, 80% and 90% of your budget.

## What happens when I run out?

The answer stops with a message that points you to the Kello team ("I need more searches, contact Kello"). Contact them and they'll sort out more budget.

<Tip>
If you send a message and Alpha doesn't reply at all, check your usage under My Account. An empty budget is the most common reason.
</Tip>

## What plans are there?

- **Free accounts** get a small monthly allowance.
- **Paid plans** are set up by the Kello team and shared across your team.

There's no public price list or self-serve checkout. To get more, or to set up a plan for your team, email **support@kello.ai** or [talk to a person](https://calendly.com/mona-gandhi-kello/ama).

## How do I make my budget go further?

- **Be specific up front.** A clear first ask saves follow-up rounds. See [finding people](/alpha/finding-people).
- **Refine the list you have** instead of starting new searches from scratch.
- **Don't resend while Alpha is working.** Answers keep running even if you close the tab. Come back and the answer is there.
- **Use stop** if Alpha is heading the wrong way. The partial answer is kept, and you can correct course.

## Is Kello (done-for-you) part of my Alpha budget?

No. Kello, the done-for-you service, is separate from Alpha usage. See the [Kello quickstart](/kello/quickstart).

## Quick reference

What Kello Alpha can't do yet, and the best way to get each job done today.

| Kello Alpha doesn't | Do this instead |
|---|---|
| Give emails or phone numbers | Reach people through your own tools, or send the role to Kello (done-for-you) |
| Benchmark salaries or filter on pay | Give your budget to Kello (done-for-you) as part of a role |
| Filter on notice period, visa or GPA | Use "open to work" as the nearest signal |
| Take file uploads | Paste the text, or a link |
| Search your applicants or ATS | Save people to a Kello collection and search inside it |
| Send outreach | Use your own tools, or let Kello's team reach out for a role |
| Remember past conversations | Keep refining in one chat, or restate key points in a new one |
| Sort, filter or bulk-select on the results page | Ask Alpha in chat |
| Show an export button | Ask "export this as CSV" |
| Show public pricing | Talk to the Kello team |

## Can I get contact details?

Not from Kello Alpha. Find the right people in Alpha, then reach them through your own channels. If you want someone to reach out for you, send the role to [Kello](/kello/quickstart); contact details are available there for any candidate you want to reach.

## Can Alpha tell me what someone earns?

No. Alpha doesn't do salary benchmarking or compensation filters. If pay matters for a hire, include your budget when you send the role to Kello (done-for-you).

## Can I filter on notice period, visa or GPA?

No. These fields aren't available to search. The nearest signal is "open to work", which Alpha can filter on. Check notice period and visa in your first conversation.

## Can I upload a JD or résumé?

Not as a file. Paste the text into the chat, or paste a link to the job post. Long pastes are fine. See [finding people](/alpha/finding-people).

## Does Alpha search my applicants or ATS?

No. Alpha searches Kello's people data, not your applicants, ATS or talent pool. The only private scope is your own Kello collections. Save people to a collection, then ask "senior engineers in my [collection name] collection". Alpha tells you how many members it can search.

## Can Alpha send messages for me?

Alpha doesn't run outreach or sequences. Contact people through your own tools. From chat, you can also connect some of your own tools and ask Alpha to draft or prepare things in them, with your approval on every write (see promise 7 below).

## Does Alpha remember my earlier chats?

No. Each conversation stands alone. Within a chat, your **Not a fit** removals and reasons sharpen the search. In a new chat, restate what matters: "no agency recruiters, product companies only".

## Can I rename, delete or share a conversation?

Not today. To share results, share a candidate with a public link, save people to a collection, or export the list as CSV.

## Can I sort or filter the results page?

Not on the page itself. Ask in chat: "only Mumbai", "drop anyone at a services company". A follow-up that returns a new list swaps the results in place.

## How do I see the rest of a long list?

Lists show 24 people per page. Use the page controls at the bottom of the results page to move through the rest.

## Where is the export button?

There isn't one yet. Ask "export this as CSV" in chat. See [working with results](/alpha/working-with-results).

## 1. It never makes up facts

Lists come from real records. Facts from the web are cited. A person dossier gives one fact and one source per line. If Alpha doesn't know something, it says so.

## 2. It tells you what it changed

If a must-have became a nice-to-have to find enough people, Alpha always says so. If something can't be filtered on, it says that in plain words and offers the nearest substitute. You never have to guess why a list looks the way it does.

## 3. It checks its own work

Before you see any people search, a second AI reviews the top results against the person you described: what fits, what leaked in, what's missing. Alpha tightens or widens the search, at most twice, then shows you the list.

## 4. Your examples are respected

When you paste LinkedIn profiles as examples, a search that would reject any one of them cannot be saved. See [finding people](/alpha/finding-people).

## 5. No discrimination by design

Alpha refuses searches on religion, caste, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, marital status or age. Where a fair substitute exists, it offers one, such as an experience band instead of an age.

For example, Alpha declines "VCs of Indian origin". It accepts "VCs in the Bay Area with prior education or work experience in India", which is about professional history, not a personal trait.

## 6. No contact scraping

Kello's data never exposes emails or phone numbers, not in search, lists or exports.

## 7. Nothing happens in your apps without your click

If you connect your own tools from chat, every send or write shows a **Needs your approval** card with the app, the action and a preview. Nothing happens without your click. **Reject** skips only that action.

## 8. It stays on task

Alpha does recruiting and talent work only. It ignores instructions hidden inside profiles, job descriptions or web pages, so a pasted document can't redirect it.

## What data does Alpha search?

Alpha searches Kello's data on 830M+ professionals and 57M companies, built from public professional information, plus your own Kello collections when you ask it to. See the [privacy policy](https://kello.ai/privacy-policy).

## Is anything thin in the data?

Work history is rich: dates, titles, seniority, tenure, and company stage and size at the time. Skills are listed on only part of profiles, and code or research signals are rare. Describe the work and career rather than leaning on skill keywords alone.
