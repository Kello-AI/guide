This Kello Alpha prompt library has six fill-in templates and 50+ copy-ready prompts, grouped by use case. Most are real asks from Kello Alpha users, and where we have it, each shows what came back. Replace anything in angle or square brackets with your own details, and refine with short follow-ups.

For why these prompts work, see [get better results](/alpha/better-results).

## Templates

Fill in the brackets and send.

**A population search**

```text
<Title or job> at <kind of company, or named companies> in <place>, <N–M> years of experience.
Must: <one or two hard needs>. Prefer: <nice-to-haves>. Exclude: <who usually leaks in>.
```

**A JD search**

```text
<paste the JD or a link to the job post>
Must-haves I won't bend on: <...>. Based in <place>. Leave out <...>.
```

**A team map**

```text
Everyone currently at <company> in <function>, grouped by seniority.
Include interns and contractors: <yes or no>.
```

**A transition or founder search**

```text
People who left <named companies, or a defined set> between <month year> and <month year>
and now <hold a Founder title / work at a Series A startup / are in stealth>.
```

**Event invites**

```text
<event link> — invite <who>, based within <area> of the venue, about <N> people.
```

**A check on the result**

```text
Why is this number so low / high? What did you leave out?
```

## Executive search: senior and niche roles

When the mandate is senior, specialist, or in finance, legal or investing.

```text
Investors currently at Principal or early Partner level at consumer-focused funds, with 12–14 years of experience. India only.
```
Use for partner-level fund searches with a tight experience band.

```text
Heads of legal or general counsels at startups that have gone public in the last 8 years
```
Use for a function at a defined set of companies. Ask for related lists one message at a time; see [one list per message](/alpha/better-results#ask-for-one-list-per-message).

```text
Heads of investor relations at India-listed banks, NBFCs and mutual funds
```
Use for a function search across a defined set of institutions.

```text
Candidates with 10+ years in a bank or NBFC, preferably in gold loans or retail secured assets, with branch audit, internal audit or fraud risk experience
```
Use when must-haves and preferences are mixed. "Preferably" ranks people higher; "must" filters.

```text
Senior investing professionals with 14+ years investing in REITs and InvITs, at funds, AMCs or similar
```
Use for niche asset-class expertise.

```text
Legal professionals at tech or IT services companies with IPR and data protection experience, based in Mumbai. Exclude general statutory compliance roles.
```
Use for a specialist within a sector and city.

```text
FP&A professionals with 5–8 years of experience in Mumbai. Good if they're at a listed company or one about to list.
```
Use when a preference should lift people, not filter them out. For what came back, see [start broad, then narrow](/alpha/better-results#start-broad-then-narrow).

## Mapping a firm or team

When you need to see who's where before a client call or BD outreach.

```text
Everyone currently at [firm name] in investing roles in India, grouped by seniority. Don't drop anyone who currently holds an investing title. Exclude fund admin, accounting and legal.
```
Use to see a firm's team by seniority before a pitch.

```text
doppel.com → give me their HR team
```
Use for one function at one company. **Real run:** 12 people in 82 s.

```text
Who are the founders? Or someone who is leading the FDE initiative?
```
Use as a follow-up to find decision-makers. Answers come back as names and roles in text. "If I'm sending shortlisted candidates for the FDE role, whom should I send it to?" got a short recommendation in 8 s.

```text
Recruiting team size of [company] in India
```
Use for a quick count of one function. **Real run:** Airtel gave 184 recruiters and 1,359 across HR, with a chart.

```text
People who have currently or previously worked at SuperKalam (YC W23)
```
Use for everyone who has passed through a company. **Real run:** 89 in 2.5 min. Say "current" or "current and former" every time.

```text
Show me early engineers of CRED
```
Use to find people who were there early. **Real run:** 174, who joined in 2018–2020.

## VC and founder hunting

When you want people to watch or back, not hire.

```text
Founding team members (first 10–15 people) from unicorns in India who have left and not yet started their own company. Examples: [2–3 LinkedIn links]
```
Use to find likely future founders, calibrated by examples. Put the links in the same message.

```text
People in stealth mode who left a top AI lab in the last 30 days
```
Use to spot new companies before they announce. Job dates are stored by month, so you get the most recent movers.

```text
People in India who have built reinforcement learning environments, in the LLM sense: task worlds with verifiers and rewards for training models
```
Use to find builders of a new kind of work. Define the term in one line.

```text
Founding team members of unicorn startups in India. Who is still there and who has left?
```
Use to map alumni of breakout companies. **Real run:** 1,312, split 59% still there and 41% left.

```text
Product managers in India with 4+ years' experience who left their job
```
Use to catch operators who may have just started something. **Real run:** 4,198, then 62 after "limit it to people who left their product roles after March 2026".

```text
Founders based in India who got acqui-hired in the last 2 years
```
Use for ex-founders ready for their next company. **Real run:** 290.

## Fundraising and LP mapping

When you're raising a fund or building an investor list.

```text
People at funds-of-funds and institutions that invest in VC funds and back emerging managers. Investing roles only; exclude retail and consulting titles.
```
Use to build a first LP list.

```text
Venture capitalists in the San Francisco Bay Area who studied in India
```
Use to find investors with a link to a market. **Real run:** 63.

```text
Heads of family offices in India with experience investing in listed or unlisted securities
```
Use to reach family-office decision-makers.

## Event guest lists

When you're filling a room.

```text
Who should we invite to our testing meetup in Indiranagar? Only people at companies with 50+ engineers.
```
Use for a local meetup with a team-size floor.

```text
Find the right people to invite to this event: [event page link]. Deeptech founders who haven't raised or just raised early.
```
Use when the event page explains the audience. **Real run:** 5,169, then 747 after "I want people who are currently in Bangalore only", then a CSV.

```text
Builders and AI-first people across Ahmedabad and Surat for an AI evening at GIFT City. Manager level or above. Capacity 30.
```
Use for a small, senior room across two cities.

```text
Here's our current invite list. Who's worth inviting for this event? [LinkedIn links]
```
Use to audit a list you already have.

## Hiring from a JD or examples

When you have an open role.

```text
Find me people for this:
[paste the job description or a link to the job post]
```
Use to start from a JD. Expect 3–10 minutes. Check Alpha's must-haves, good-to-haves and assumptions before you go further; refinements take about a minute each.

```text
Find more people like these. Based in Bengaluru, open to an early-stage startup. [up to 10 LinkedIn links]
```
Use when you know good when you see it.

```text
Backend engineers with 3–5 years' experience who've worked at a YC-backed startup, based in Bengaluru
```
Use for a precise early-stage engineering search.

```text
People who joined Razorpay when it had fewer than 100 employees
```
Use to find people who've seen a company scale.

```text
Engineering managers in Hyderabad who have never worked at TCS or Infosys
```
Use when exclusions matter as much as inclusions.

## Career moves

When the move itself is the signal.

```text
People who left Google for a startup
```
Use to find big-company talent already comfortable at startups.

```text
Engineers who worked at Swiggy between 2014 and 2018
```
Use to find people from a specific era at a company. A named company plus a date range is one of the most reliable asks. **Real runs:** 454–544 for 2014–2018; 172 for 2015–2016, in about a minute.

```text
Show me more people like [one person from the list]
```
Use as a follow-up on a list you like. **Real run:** 217 Swiggy engineers who went on to found or lead startups.

```text
People who joined Stripe from a bank
```
Use to find people who've made a specific industry jump.

## Company and market intelligence

When the question is about companies or the market, not one person.

```text
Startups in India likely to go public in the next 3–5 years, and roles we could target. Say why each is on the list.
```
Use for BD lists for a search firm. Alpha uses the web plus its data here.

```text
Companies with an office in Hyderabad, HQ outside India, India team of 10–100, actively hiring in Hyderabad
```
Use to find global companies building teams locally. **Real run:** 22 companies in 8 minutes. Asking for "India team size and number of roles they are hiring for" gave those columns as a table in chat; computed columns like these don't go into the CSV file.

```text
Where did Series A startups' first PMs come from?
```
Use for a talent-origin question. **Real run:** about 15,000 PM stints; 38% were already PMs, 12% engineers, 9% former founders, with a chart.

```text
Show me the job market trend in Bengaluru for 2026. What are the top job titles, and the rare but new ones?
```
Use for a market read. **Real run:** 420,000+ postings by quarter, plus emerging titles such as AI agent engineer and forward deployed engineer.

```text
How many PhDs does Baseten have?
```
Use for one number. **Real run:** 8, in text; "show me all 8" gave the list.

```text
Where do Swiggy engineers go next?
```
Use to see talent flow and who you compete with for people.

```text
How many senior data engineers in Pune have fintech experience?
```
Use to size a market before you commit to a search.

```text
Open senior backend roles at fintechs in Bangalore posted this month
```
Use for "who's hiring for X" and competitive intel.

## References and person research

When you're about to make a decision on one person.

```text
I want to do a reference check on [LinkedIn link]
```
Use to find who actually worked with a candidate. **Real run:** the 8 best people to call in about 3 minutes, with a note when a pair moved jobs together.

```text
Prep me for the call with [reference name]
```
Use before each reference call. A call script comes back in about 10 s.

```text
Tell me about close seconds whom you considered and rejected, and why
```
Use for backup references.

```text
Tell me everything about [name] of [company], including web search
```
Use for a short, sourced summary of one person, in 1–2 minutes.

```text
What pitch can I make to her? I have a call scheduled with her.
```
Use as a follow-up to prepare a sales call. Answers come back in about 20 s.

## Connected apps

When you want work emails or drafts through your own tools, such as Apollo, Gmail or FullEnrich. Anything that sends or writes waits for your approval.

```text
Can I connect my Apollo account?
```
Use to get a secure connect link. Never paste an API key into chat.

```text
Use Apollo to find their work emails
```
Use after a list. Work emails only; personal emails and phone numbers are refused.

```text
Draft an email to reach out to [person]. Then push it as a draft to my Gmail.
```
Use to turn a list into outreach you send yourself. See [usage, limits and trust](/alpha/usage-limits-and-trust).

## Follow-ups and export

After any list.

```text
Only principals. No founders or partners.
```
Use to tighten seniority.

```text
Out of the above, show me those in real estate
```
Use to narrow within the current list.

```text
Can we narrow it down to the top 10-15?
```
Use when you want a shortlist. See [ask for a number](/alpha/better-results#ask-for-a-number-when-you-want-a-shortlist).

```text
Export this as CSV with name, current title, company, location and LinkedIn URL
```
Use to take the list into a spreadsheet. The link comes back in about 15 s. See [export](/alpha/working-with-results).

```text
Search my [collection name] collection for people with fintech experience
```
Use to search people you've already saved. "Share my collections as a table" lists them.
