Kello Alpha answers company and market questions as well as people questions. Ask for a list of companies, a deep-dive on one company, where a company's people come from and go next, how its headcount has changed, or how many people with a given background exist in a market, and Kello Alpha answers with real numbers, charts and sources.

## How do I get a list of companies?

Describe the companies:

```text
Voice-AI startups in Bangalore
```

```text
Series B fintechs in India with 100–500 people
```

```text
Companies with an office in Hyderabad, HQ outside India, India team of 10–100, actively hiring in Hyderabad
```

You get a list with industry, headcount, HQ, founded year and funding stage. Each company opens a full company page. Ask "also tell me why each one is on the list" and Alpha adds a reason per company.

## What's on a company page?

A company page shows what Kello knows about one company. Sections include:

- **Basics**: founded, stage, employees, funding, investors
- **Notable people** and **skip-level leaders**
- **Headcount trend** over time
- **Org structure** and **seniority mix**
- **Tenure and stickiness**: how long people stay
- **Top locations**
- **Feeder schools**
- **Talent flow**: where people came from and where they went, filterable by function

Each section appears only when there's data for that company, so smaller companies show fewer sections.

For colleges, the page shows notable alumni, alumni outcomes and the functions alumni work in.

## How do I see talent flow?

Ask where people come from or go next:

```text
Where do Swiggy engineers go next?
```

```text
Where did Series A startups' first PMs come from?
```

Talent flow is useful for finding companies to source from, and for understanding who you compete with for people.

## How do I check a company's headcount trend?

Ask for it. Alpha shows month-by-month headcount for up to 5 years for any company. Use it to spot growth, distress or a company winding down.

```text
Is this candidate's current employer shrinking? [company name]
```

```text
How many people joined [company] in the last 6 months?
```

A shrinking employer can be a good reason to reach out to someone.

## Can Alpha answer market questions with numbers?

Yes. Alpha counts real profiles and shows the answer as a number, a chart or a small table.

```text
How many senior data engineers in Pune have fintech experience?
```

```text
How are Stripe's Bangalore engineers spread across seniority?
```

```text
How many ML engineers in India have a CS degree from BITS?
```

## How do I map a firm's team?

Ask Alpha to map it. This is common for executive search and BD work.

```text
Map the India investing team of [firm name]
```

```text
Recruiting team size of [company] in India
```

## Can Alpha search job postings?

Yes. Alpha searches millions of live job postings. Use it for "who is hiring for X" and competitive intel.

```text
Open senior backend roles at fintechs in Bangalore posted this month
```

Postings are shown in the answer. They aren't saved as a list.

## Can Alpha research beyond its database?

Yes. For things the database doesn't hold, such as funding news, leadership changes, event pages or what a new term means, Alpha searches the web and reads up to 20 pages at once. It cites its sources.

```text
Give me a 5-line brief on [company website]
```

## What's a person dossier?

A short, sourced profile of one person. Ask "tell me everything about [name] from [company]". Alpha first confirms it has the right person and says if there's a namesake. Then it returns identity, career, education, public work and recruiter-relevant signals, with one fact and one source per line. A dossier never includes contact details.
