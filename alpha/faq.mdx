Kello Alpha is Kello's self-serve AI search app for people and companies. These are the questions Kello Alpha users ask most, with short answers and links to the full guide.

## Results

### Can I download my results?

Yes. Type "export this as CSV" in the chat. Alpha replies with a download link that works for 30 minutes, with up to 5,000 rows per file. You can ask for specific columns. There's no export button on the results page yet. See [export](/alpha/working-with-results).

### Why don't I see all N people?

The results page shows 24 people per page. Use the page controls at the bottom to see the rest. A list can hold up to 1,000 people.

### Why is this person on my list?

Open their profile and look at **N / M matched**. It lists every criterion from your ask, ticked or not, with a one-line proof, and highlights the jobs that matched. If they match fewer than all criteria, check Alpha's answer: it always says when it loosened a must-have. See [working with results](/alpha/working-with-results).

### How do I get rid of people who don't fit?

Click thumbs-down on the profile, say what's off, and choose **Remove from this list**. They leave the list, its exports and every refined version of the search. Alpha reads your reasons on your next message.

### Can I sort or filter the results page?

Not on the page. Ask Alpha in chat, for example "only people in Mumbai". The new list replaces the old one in place.

### Can a colleague see my list?

Share a candidate with a public link, save people to a Kello collection, or export the list as CSV and share the file. Conversations can't be shared yet.

## Searching

### Why did Alpha refuse my search?

Most likely because it asked for a personal trait. Alpha won't search on religion, caste, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, marital status or age. Rephrase around professional history: "prior education or work experience in India" instead of "of Indian origin", or an experience band instead of an age. See [trust](/alpha/usage-limits-and-trust).

### Why are some results not quite what I asked for?

Look-alike job titles can leak in, such as investor relations in an investing search. Tell Alpha exactly what to drop: "only investing roles, no investor relations". Remove the misses with **Not a fit** and give a reason.

### Why did Alpha only search my country?

Alpha searches your home country by default and says so. Reply "search globally", or name the places you want.

### Can I upload a JD or résumé file?

Not yet. Paste the text or a link to the job post. See [hire from a JD](/alpha/finding-people).

### Does Alpha search my ATS or applicants?

No. It searches Kello's people data and, when you ask, your own Kello collections.

### Can I close the tab while Alpha works?

Yes. Alpha keeps working. Come back and the answer is there.

## Contact details and outreach

### Can I get emails or phone numbers?

No. Kello Alpha doesn't provide contact details, in search, lists or exports. Reach people through your own tools. If you want contact details or want someone to reach out for you, send the role to [Kello](/kello/quickstart), the done-for-you service.

### Can Alpha send outreach?

No. Alpha finds and researches people. For outreach on a role, Kello's team can reach out on your behalf through the done-for-you service.

## Usage

### Does Alpha use my budget?

Yes. Your budget measures the AI work Alpha does on your messages, not a count of searches. Bigger asks use more. See it under **My Account** in **Usage**. Kello emails you at 50%, 80% and 90% used. See [usage](/alpha/usage-limits-and-trust).

### What if I run out?

Alpha stops with a message. Contact the Kello team at **support@kello.ai** for more. If Alpha doesn't reply at all to a message, check your usage first.

### How much does Kello Alpha cost?

Free accounts get a small monthly allowance. Paid plans are set up by the Kello team and shared across a team. There's no public price list; email **support@kello.ai** or [talk to a person](https://calendly.com/mona-gandhi-kello/ama).

## Kello Alpha and Kello

### What's the difference between Kello Alpha and Kello?

Kello Alpha is the app you drive: you search, explore and export. Kello is the done-for-you service: send a role and get up to 20 people with reasons they fit, and Kello's team reaches out to the ones you approve. See the [overview](/alpha/overview).

### How many people can Alpha search?

830M+ professionals in 200+ countries, 57M companies, and millions of live job postings.
